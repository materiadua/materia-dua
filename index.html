About

From image
to object.

Materia Dua creates tactile objects that explore the dual nature of art as image and function.

The first collection translates original imagery into embroidered cushions — designed, digitally embroidered and hand sewn by Maria Cepeda.

Each piece sits somewhere between artwork and useful object, with the material, colour and stitch work becoming part of the image itself.
